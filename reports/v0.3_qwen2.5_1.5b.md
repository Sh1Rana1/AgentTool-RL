## Metric definitions

- **Success:** correct diagnosis, required evidence, and accepted mitigation.
- **Schema valid:** calls that pass tool name, type, required-field, and enum checks.
- **Semantic valid:** executable calls divided by schema-valid calls.
- **Coverage:** required evidence collected before the episode ends.
- **Evidence valid:** submitted references that were observed earlier in the episode.
- **Recovery:** recoverable errors immediately corrected with the same tool.
- **Invalid:** calls rejected by schema or semantic validation.

The rule-based agent encodes heuristics for the five known template families and is an environment sanity-check, not a learned generalization result.
